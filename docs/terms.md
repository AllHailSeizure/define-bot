## 2. What the app does

Mention `u/define-bot` with `!define [term]` in a comment, and the app replies with a short excerpt and link from the matching English Wikipedia article. It uses Google Gemini to pick the article title.

## 3. Accuracy

The term-to-article match is chosen by an AI model and can be wrong. Wikipedia content can be incomplete or inaccurate. Excerpts are shortened and are not a substitute for the full article or for expert sources. Excerpt text comes from Wikipedia under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), and each reply links the source article.
